Outbound internet connections · Tanium 15-day report, approx. Sept 1–16, 2026

Internet Destinations by Site

Where application servers and Citrix servers at each site send their outbound internet connections. Proxy rows are connections made to a proxy; every other row is a direct connection to that destination. Zscaler counts connections landing in Zscaler's IP ranges. All counts are 15-day totals (divide by 15 for a daily average).

Application servers are servers with an APM ID. Zscaler App Connectors and servers with no APM ID are left out.

Site is where the server's IP sits in IPAM: Aetna is Middletown and Windsor; CVS is Shea, RI One and Cumberland 2100 Highland.

Time window: Tanium export 15d_report (drop 9-16-26), covering about Sept 1–16, 2026. The export has no timestamps, so traffic cannot be split by day.

Destination is the network owner of the IP address. Tanium records connections, not bytes, and cannot see traffic redirected after it leaves the server (for example by WCCP).
